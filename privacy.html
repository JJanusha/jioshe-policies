JioShe Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 14, 2026

INTRODUCTION

JioShe ("we," "our," or "us") is a women's health and wellness application developed by an individual developer in Sri Lanka. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal health information with care and respect.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the JioShe mobile application.

Contact: jioshe0204@gmail.com

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

1.1 Health Data You Provide

When you use JioShe, you may provide:

  • Menstrual cycle information (period dates, cycle length, flow intensity)
  • PCOS symptoms and tracking data
  • Medications and supplements
  • Physical health metrics (weight, BMI, height)
  • Daily logs (mood, symptoms, water intake, sleep quality)
  • Breast health self-examination records
  • Health goals and wellness preferences
  • Emergency contact information (Health Passport feature)

1.2 Images (Temporary Processing Only)

  • Breast health guidance images: Processed temporarily by AI, NEVER stored permanently, deleted immediately after analysis
  • Skin/symptom photos: For AI analysis, processed temporarily and deleted after analysis
  • No human ever views these images - All processing is automated via AI

1.3 Account Information

  • Email address (for account login and communication)
  • Date of birth (year only, for age verification under COPPA/GDPR)
  • Display name (optional)
  • Profile picture (optional)

1.4 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device information (device type, operating system version)
  • App usage data (features used, time spent in app)
  • Log data (crash reports, error logs for debugging)

1.5 AI Interaction Data (If You Opt In)

  • Questions you ask Serah AI health chatbot
  • Anonymized health patterns for personalized cycle predictions
  • Wellness recommendations and insights

2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

2.1 Core App Functions

  • Track and predict menstrual cycles with AI algorithms
  • Provide PCOS symptom tracking and pattern identification
  • Calculate BMI and health metrics
  • Send medication reminders and period predictions via notifications
  • Generate personalized health insights and recommendations
  • Enable breast health self-examination guidance

2.2 AI-Powered Features (Optional - Requires Your Consent)

  • Personalize menstrual cycle predictions using machine learning models
  • Identify PCOS symptom patterns and correlations
  • Provide wellness recommendations through Serah AI chatbot (powered by Google Gemini)
  • Your data is anonymized (personal identifiers removed) and NEVER used to train public AI models like ChatGPT or Gemini

2.3 Future Telemedicine (Not Yet Available)

  • When implemented, we will connect you with verified healthcare professionals in Sri Lanka
  • Your health data will be shared with doctors ONLY with your explicit consent for each consultation
  • All consultations will be confidential

3. HOW WE PROTECT YOUR INFORMATION

3.1 Encryption

  • All health data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS
  • Data stored in Firebase Cloud Firestore is encrypted at rest
  • Your password is hashed and never stored in plain text

3.2 Access Controls

  • Only YOU can access your health data through your authenticated account
  • No JioShe staff, developers, or support personnel can view your personal health information
  • AI processing is fully automated with zero human review

3.3 Breast Health Images - Special Protection

  • Processed by AI algorithm ONLY using Google Gemini Vision API
  • Analysis completes in seconds
  • Images are DELETED immediately after AI processing completes
  • NEVER stored on any server
  • NEVER transmitted to humans or support staff
  • NEVER used for training, research, or any other purpose

4. DATA SHARING AND THIRD PARTIES

4.1 We DO NOT Sell Your Data

Your health information is NEVER sold to data brokers, advertisers, pharmaceutical companies, insurance companies, social media platforms, or any third party.

4.2 Service Providers We Use

We use the following trusted third-party services:

  • Google Firebase: Secure cloud storage, authentication, and database
  • Google Gemini AI: AI-powered health insights, chatbot, and image analysis
  • Firebase Cloud Functions: Serverless backend operations

These services are bound by strict privacy agreements and process data only as needed for app functionality.

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required by valid court orders, government authorities, or law enforcement.

5. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

5.1 Right to Access

  • View all your health data within the app at any time
  • Request a complete copy of your data via email: jioshe0204@gmail.com

5.2 Right to Correct

  • Edit or update your information anytime in the app

5.3 Right to Delete

  • Delete your account and all associated data through Settings
  • Request deletion via email: jioshe0204@gmail.com
  • Data is permanently deleted from all systems within 30 days

5.4 Right to Export

  • Request a copy of your data in machine-readable format
  • Contact: jioshe0204@gmail.com

5.5 Right to Opt Out

  • Disable AI insights anytime in Settings
  • Opt out of push notifications in device settings

6. DATA RETENTION

  • Active accounts: Data stored as long as your account exists
  • Deleted accounts: Data permanently deleted within 30 days
  • Backup retention: Deleted data removed from backups within 90 days
  • Breast health images: NEVER stored (deleted immediately)

7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

JioShe is intended for users aged 13 and older. We comply with COPPA and international standards.

  • We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13
  • Age verification is required during signup
  • If we discover a user is under 13, the account will be deleted

8. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS

Your data is stored on Google Firebase servers, which may be located outside Sri Lanka. We ensure appropriate safeguards:

  • Google complies with GDPR and international privacy frameworks
  • Data transfers use standard contractual clauses
  • All transfers are encrypted

9. DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION

In the unlikely event of a data breach:

  • We will notify you within 72 hours via email
  • We will report to relevant authorities as required by law
  • We will take immediate action to secure systems

10. COMPLIANCE WITH PRIVACY LAWS

JioShe complies with:

  • GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation - EU)
  • PDPA (Personal Data Protection Act - Sri Lanka)
  • HIPAA principles (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act - USA)
  • COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)

11. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect:

  • New app features or functionality
  • Changes in legal requirements

Changes will be posted in the app with the "Last Updated" date. Continued use after changes indicates acceptance.

12. YOUR CONSENT

By using JioShe, you consent to this Privacy Policy and our data practices.

We provide tools for self-tracking and education but do NOT provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

  • For emergencies, call 1990 (Sri Lanka Emergency Services)
  • Breast health image analysis is for educational awareness only
